# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — 2026-11-02

Initial public release.

- Local, text-only OpenAI Chat Completions gateway for one GGUF model.
- Durable conversation recording and a compact conversation-first Studio.
- Context Effects with linked support and suppression, answer lenses, heatmaps, and ranked highlights.
- Bounded Lean Context with redline, kept-only context, and fresh before/after generations.
- Recorded interactive demo and source installer for macOS and Linux.

The supported boundary and known exclusions are documented in
[docs/CAPABILITIES.md](docs/CAPABILITIES.md).
